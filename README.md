# SEKOOL Content

Marketing and content assets for [SEKOOL.my](https://sekool.my): 1-to-1 online classes for primary school students (KSSR/KSSM) with Cikgu from Sekolah Kebangsaan.

| Project | What it is |
|---------|-----------|
| [`sekool-explainer/`](sekool-explainer/) | A 48-second premium explainer for parents (1080p60, music + SFX). The rendered video is at [`sekool-explainer/output/sekool-explainer.mp4`](sekool-explainer/output/sekool-explainer.mp4). |
| [`sekool-ugc/`](sekool-ugc/) | A 43-second 9:16 green-screen UGC reel (Malay script, word-by-word captions). The draft with a placeholder avatar is at [`sekool-ugc/output/sekool-ugc-draft.mp4`](sekool-ugc/output/sekool-ugc-draft.mp4). Drop a green-screen avatar clip into `sekool-ugc/avatar/` to render the final. |

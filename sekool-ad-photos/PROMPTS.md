# SEKOOL ad photos: "shot on a parent's iPhone" prompt pack

Copy-paste prompts for making candid, iPhone-style photos for SEKOOL ads, across many different kids, parents, homes and Cikgu. The prompts are based on our real customer photos: over-the-shoulder class shots at home, results-day selfies and celebration meals.

Every prompt already includes the style. Paste one into the image tool and generate.

---

## 1. Read this before using the photos in ads

**Class-at-home shots (Section A) and Cikgu shots (Section D) are safe to use as lifestyle images.** They show what a SEKOOL class looks like, not a claim about results.

**Results-day and celebration shots (Sections B and C) need care.** An AI-made child holding a report slip or a "No. 1 Dalam Kelas" plaque, placed in a SEKOOL ad, tells parents "this is one of our students and these are their results." That child doesn't exist, so the ad would be a false testimonial. This can breach Meta's ad policies and Malaysia's Consumer Protection Act 1999 (false or misleading representations). Recommended:

- For **results or proof ads**, use **real** customer photos with the parent's written consent, like the ones we already have. Blur names and IC numbers as we did in the originals.
- Use AI versions of B and C only as **mood images with no results claim**, for example a headline like *"Moments like this start at home"* with no marks, rankings, "our student" or quotes.
- Never put a made-up name, school or quote on an AI face.
- Keep all text on slips, certificates and plaques **unreadable** in AI images. The prompts below already ask for this.

## 2. Which tool to use

| Tool | Good at | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **ChatGPT (GPT image)** | Following long prompts, text on screens, attached reference photos | Best all-rounder for this pack |
| **Gemini (Nano Banana)** | Realistic people and homes, editing a real photo ("change the child, keep everything else") | Great for making variations of our real photos |
| **Midjourney v7** | The most realistic photos | Screen text comes out as gibberish, so plan to swap the screen (see tip 2) |

**Tip 1: attach a real photo as a style reference.** This gives the most convincing result. Attach one of our original photos and add this line before the prompt:

> Use the attached photo only as a reference for camera style, lighting, colour and composition. Create a completely new child, new room and new people. Do not copy any face.

**Tip 2: swap in the real SEKOOL screen.** AI tools cannot draw our class screen accurately. For ads, generate the photo, then paste a real screenshot of a SEKOOL class onto the laptop screen with Canva (Frames) or Photoshop (Edit, Transform, Distort). Lower the screenshot's opacity to about 95% and add slight blur so it matches the photo. Our logo and interface then look exactly right.

**Sizes for Meta:** 4:5 (1080×1350) for Feed, 9:16 (1080×1920) for Stories and Reels, 1:1 as a fallback. Change the ratio at the end of any prompt.

## 3. What to reuse in your own prompts

**Style (at the end of every prompt):**
> Candid snapshot taken by a parent on an iPhone, handheld and slightly tilted, main 26mm wide lens, everything in focus with no background blur, natural mixed indoor light (window daylight plus warm ceiling lights), mild iPhone HDR look, slight sensor noise, true-to-life colours, ordinary lived-in Malaysian home with everyday clutter. Unstaged, not a stock photo, no studio lighting, no retouching, no text overlay.

**Class screen (used in Sections A and D):**
> The screen shows an online video class: a large white digital whiteboard with neat handwritten working in blue, red and green pen, and two small video tiles on the right. The top tile is the Cikgu with a small blue "SEKOOL" logo in the corner, and the bottom tile is the child's own webcam. Call controls sit along the bottom with a red hang-up button.

## 4. Checklist before posting

Look for these tell-tale AI mistakes and regenerate or fix them:

- [ ] **Hands:** five fingers each, and the pen held naturally
- [ ] **Screen:** no gibberish text. If there is any, swap in a real screenshot (tip 2).
- [ ] **Keyboard:** keys look normal, no melted or duplicated rows
- [ ] **Tudung:** drapes naturally with no hair poking through strangely and no odd seams
- [ ] **Uniforms:** look like real Malaysian school uniforms
- [ ] **Faces:** real skin texture, not plastic or airbrushed
- [ ] **Papers, plaques and certificates:** text unreadable, with no real names, IC numbers or school names
- [ ] **Brands:** no readable third-party logos (restaurants, laptop makers' big logos, mall shops)
- [ ] **Faces:** don't resemble a real person (celebrity, customer or staff member)
- [ ] **Claims:** the ad copy makes no results claim on AI images from Sections B and C

## 5. Making more variations

Swap any of these into a prompt to get new avatars:

- **Child:** Malay / Chinese / Indian / Kadazan-Dusun / Iban / Orang Asli / mixed-race; age 7–12; boy or girl; tudung or no tudung; glasses; braces; chubby or slim
- **Adult nearby:** mum, dad, nenek, atuk, older sibling, maid or helper, nobody
- **Home:** condo, terrace house, flat or PPR, kampung house, semi-D, shophouse apartment, grandparents' house
- **Spot:** dining table, floor, sofa, bedroom desk, kitchen counter, balcony, car (parked)
- **Time:** morning, after school, evening, night
- **Device:** MacBook, Windows laptop, Android tablet, iPad, class cast to TV
- **Subject on screen:** Matematik (addition, fractions, percentages, shapes), Sains (plants, magnets, the moon), Bahasa Melayu (suku kata, karangan), English (spelling, grammar), Sejarah
- **Cikgu:** male or female; Malay, Chinese or Indian; tudung colours

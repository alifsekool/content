1. **Year range.** You said Year 1 – Year 6, but sekool.my and `llms.txt` currently say **Darjah 2 – Darjah 6**. The video uses "Year 1 – Year 6" in two places (scene 3 pill and scene 9 subtitle). Change them in `src/index.html` if needed.
2. **Illustrative data.** Adam, Cikgu Aina, the TP2 → TP4 → TP5 journey, the star ratings and the fractions question are example content, not real results or claims.
3. **Stage shown.** Scene 5 shows the Stage 2 (Intermediate) report with "Guided learning". The Stage 1 method is *Referencing* and the Stage 3 method is *KBAT practice*, taken from the PDFs. Swap them if you'd rather feature a different stage.
